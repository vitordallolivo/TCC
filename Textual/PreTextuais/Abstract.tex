\begin{resumo}[Abstract]
 \begin{otherlanguage*}{english}
   This work develops an integrated acquisition system for the Albatroz Aerodesign test bench for electric propulsion assemblies based on BLDC motors. A measurement board, based on the CH32V307 microcontroller, acquires the motor phase voltages and currents, drives the motor controller and the power relays and handles safety, communicating over Ethernet and UDP with a supervisory \textit{software}. To estimate the shaft angular velocity and torque without mechanical sensors, a sliding mode observer (SMO), combined with a PLL and the analytical reconstruction of the back-electromotive force, runs embedded on the board. The system enables the comparison of measured quantities with those estimated by the observer, serving as a characterization tool for electric propulsion assemblies.
   
   \textbf{Keywords}: Test Bench. BLDC Motor. Sliding Mode Observer. Data Acquisition. \textit{Sensorless} System. Electric Propulsion.
 \end{otherlanguage*}
\end{resumo}
